Case 01

DutchBasecamp

Startup internationalisation

Netherlands

The Challenge

DutchBasecamp wanted to help its startups and scaleups explore expansion into India while reducing market-entry risk and deepening their understanding of the local market.

How GLB Helped

GLB delivered India market insights, market validation, ecosystem introductions, partner discovery, business matchmaking, and founder mentoring — including go-to-market training on cultural adaptation, GTM strategy, research and validation, and investor readiness.

The Outcome

Between August 2021 and November 2023, GLB coached and prepared 163 startups from 27 countries across 17 verticals — one of DutchBasecamp's most well-received internationalisation programmes, with an average participant satisfaction of 9 out of 10. Founders left with stronger India market readiness, faster access to local networks, and far less uncertainty about whether and how to enter.

Case 02

CBNagro.tech

AgriTech · post-harvest storage technology

Romania

The Challenge

CBNagro.tech, which builds IoT-enabled monitoring and automation for grain storage, wanted to evaluate the Indian agriculture market and understand the real potential for its post-harvest technology there.

How GLB Helped

GLB ran a full India market assessment — agritech ecosystem research, customer and stakeholder identification, local partner exploration, and go-to-market and business-development guidance — culminating in a detailed readiness assessment of the opportunity.

The Outcome

GLB gave CBNagro a clear, well-evidenced picture of the Indian post-harvest ecosystem, the openings for its solution, and the practical barriers to entry. With that clarity — and access to local insights and networks — the company was able to make a confident, informed call on timing rather than commit prematurely.

"Working with Global Launch Base and team gave us plenty of useful insights on the Indian market in a very short period of time, and helped us form meaningful connections within the country. Their enthusiasm and commitment has been of great help!"

Ioana Ștefan · CBN-Agro Tech
Case 03

Nano-Tech SpA

Advanced composite materials & nanotechnology

Italy

The Challenge

Nano-Tech, which develops patented composite and nanomaterials for high-performance aerospace and automotive applications, wanted to evaluate the Indian market, identify strategic partners, gauge customer demand, and explore local talent and business development for future expansion.

How GLB Helped

GLB delivered India market research and opportunity assessment, industry ecosystem mapping, partner identification and introductions, business-development support, local hiring and talent exploration, and market-entry strategy guidance — producing an in-depth market study and a practical entry roadmap.

The Outcome

Nano-Tech gained a comprehensive view of the Indian market for advanced composites — demand drivers, priority sectors, potential local partners, and routes to hiring — alongside a clear market-entry roadmap to inform its expansion planning.

Case 04

Startup mentoring & market expansion · European Institute of Innovation and Technology

European Union

Case 06

3minutesjob.com

International recruitment platform

Romania
