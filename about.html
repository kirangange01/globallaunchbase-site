About

Operators, not advisors.

Global Launch Base helps European companies enter India. We were built by operators who have done this work from both sides — running European businesses in India and Indian businesses in Europe.

We work hands-on. We don't write strategy decks and walk away. We co-build the entity, the team, the compliance setup, and the first commercial agreements. When you scale, we hand over cleanly — or stay on as advisors.

We're based in Amsterdam, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru, with on-ground capability across India. We've coached over 160 startups through DutchBaseCamp and worked with European ventures across agritech, retail technology, biotech, and clean energy.
