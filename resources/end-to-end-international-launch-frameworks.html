End-to-End International Launch Framework
 Empowering Startups & Enterprises to Go Global

Who We Are:

Global Launch Base (GLB) helps businesses expand into international markets by providing a comprehensive, step-by-step framework. From assessing market readiness to guiding post-launch optimization, we support businesses throughout the entire international launch process. Our expert strategies and tailored solutions ensure a smooth, efficient transition into global markets.

Our End-to-End International Launch Framework

1. Market Readiness Assessment

  • Business Model & Product Validation
  • IP, Regulatory & Legal Compliance Checks
  • Product-Market Fit Analysis

2. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy Design

  • Target Market Selection (via Data & Trends)
  • Competitive Analysis & Value Proposition Crafting
  • Channel Strategy (Online, Offline, Hybrid)

3. Localization & Adaptation

  • Language & Cultural Customization
  • Pricing Optimization & Distribution Logistics
  • Website, UX/UI, Packaging & Documentation Adjustments

4. Partner & Investor Connect

  • Introductions to Local Distributors & Agents
  • VC/Angel/Fund Networks in Target Regions
  • Collaboration with Incubators & Trade Bodies

5. Soft-Landing Support

  • Business Setup (Entity, Tax, Banking)
  • Office/Coworking Space Setup
  • HR & Hiring Assistance

6. Launch & Post-Launch Support

  • Local Events, Expos, and Demo Days
  • PR, Influencer & Media Strategy
  • Customer Success, Feedback Loop & Iteration
